## `/cases` — results

New. This is the single largest gap on the site: a buyer of ERP asks one question before
any other, which is who else like me did this and had it work. Twenty three audited
pages answer it nowhere.

Cases are anonymous with real numbers, per the agreed approach. The hub lists them; each
has its own page.

Case page template, in order: the profile block, the situation before, the scope
delivered with named modules, the metrics before and after, the elapsed time, and a CTA.
The identification block is kept structurally separate from the metrics so a case can be
named later without reworking the page.

## `/faq` — frequently asked questions

Kept, with good coverage across thirty eight questions. Four fixes:

**Resolve the fiscal contradiction.** Today the feature map and the editions page state
that the Brazilian fiscal localisation is complete and mature, while the FAQ says fiscal
requirements are not part of the core and may require integration with a specialist
fiscal product. One position, and it should be the most detailed and honest one, because
this is where the company has real authority.

**Answer the price question.** The word "premises" appears more than ten times in the
commercial block, which turns every price question into a process answer. The reader
asks what it costs and hears that it depends. Ranges and a minimum contract go in,
linking `/investimento`.

**Add the FAQ structured data** so the answers can surface in search.

**Give it a CTA.** The closing block today asks whether the reader is ready and offers
no button.

## `/internacional` — international services

New, in Portuguese in this phase; the English site is roadmap.

Abroad the offer is deliberately not new implementations. Four lines, each with a named
deliverable, a typical duration and a contract format.

| Line | EN                                    | PT                                     |
| ---- | ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| 1    | OCA grade development                 | Desenvolvimento no padrão OCA          |
| 2    | Sustaining and application management | Sustentação e gestão da aplicação      |
| 3    | Standardisation and de-customisation  | Padronização e remoção de customização |
| 4    | Migration and upgrade                 | Migração e upgrade                     |

The positioning line:

| EN                                                            | PT                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The same team that reviews the community's code writes yours. | A mesma equipe que revisa o código da comunidade escreve o seu. |

A note recorded here so the geography is not chosen on sentiment: the Portuguese
speaking hypothesis is weak on evidence. The Portuguese localisation repository is small
and concentrated on a single maintainer, and the other Portuguese speaking markets have
almost no community localisation at all. Shared language does not come with a mature
ecosystem or with volume. If the real criteria are low fiscal complexity, a strong
community presence and demand for sustaining, the rational targets are Spain, Italy,
France and Belgium, where the network already knows the name from the reviews — and the
entry route is subcontracting for other community partners rather than direct sales to
end clients. Portugal works better as a cultural bridgehead than as a primary market.

The page therefore leads with the service lines and the reviewing credential, and does
not claim a geographic footprint that does not exist yet.
